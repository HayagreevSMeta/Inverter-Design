Conclusion

800V architecture is justified by ultra-fast charging speed, reduced cable weight, and overall efficiency gains.
FC topology was selected over NPC for its superior thermal symmetry and natural balancing capabilities under dynamic EV loads.
Adaptive fsw schedule: Achieved a 75% switching loss reduction in urban driving, saving 190W (3-phase) compared to a fixed 20 kHz baseline.
Thermal benefit: Delivered a 9.7°C Tj reduction and 53% less thermal cycling, directly extending the power module's fatigue lifetime.
All metrics validated: Time-domain simulation confirmed THD < 2%, FC balance < 0.04V error, and Tj < 54°C with >120°C thermal margin.
Future work: Experimental validation of the adaptive schedule and thermal models on a hardware prototype.
