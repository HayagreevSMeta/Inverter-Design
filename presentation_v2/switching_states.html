FC Switching States and PD-PWM

Single phase leg structure: Four switches (S1-S4) connected in series, with the flying capacitor (Cfc) connected between the S1/S2 and S3/S4 junctions.
Output voltage levels: The topology synthesizes three distinct voltage levels at the output node: +Vdc/2, 0, and -Vdc/2.
Redundant zero states enable natural balancing:
State 1: S1=1, S2=0, S3=1, S4=0 → Output = 0V (Charges Cfc)
State 2: S1=0, S2=1, S3=0, S4=1 → Output = 0V (Discharges Cfc)
PD-PWM modulation: Phase Disposition PWM actively selects between the redundant zero states based on the current direction to maintain Vfc at exactly Vdc/2 (400V).
