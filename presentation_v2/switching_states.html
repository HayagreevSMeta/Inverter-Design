FC Switching States and PD-PWM

3-Phase Structure: Each phase leg (a, b, c) utilizes four switches (e.g., q1a, q2a, q2a, q1a) with a flying capacitor bridging the inner junctions.
Output voltage levels: The topology synthesizes three distinct voltage levels per phase relative to the DC midpoint (0): +Vdc, 0, and -Vdc.
Redundant zero states enable natural balancing:
State 1: q1=1, q2=0 → Output = 0V (Charges Cfc)
State 2: q1=0, q2=1 → Output = 0V (Discharges Cfc)
PD-PWM modulation: Phase Disposition PWM actively selects between the redundant zero states based on the phase current direction to maintain Vfc at exactly Vdc/2.
Source: Advanced Power Electronics Converters (E. Cipriano)
