Why 800V Architecture?

Ultra-fast charging at 350+ kW — reduces charge time from ~45 min to under 20 min compared to 400V systems.
75% reduction in I²R losses — halving current for the same power output cuts ohmic losses across inverter, motor, and cabling.
50% less copper — smaller conductor cross-section saves approximately 10–15 kg of vehicle weight.
Industry adoption — Porsche Taycan, Hyundai E-GMP, and GM Ultium all use 800V platforms.
Challenge: Higher DC-link voltage significantly increases dv/dt stress on motor insulation, demanding advanced inverter topologies.
