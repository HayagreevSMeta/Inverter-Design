The dv/dt Problem and the Multilevel Solution

The 800V Challenge
A standard 2-level VSI at 800V switches the full bus voltage in under 50 ns, generating dv/dt spikes exceeding 16 kV/µs.
Standard motor insulation is rated for ~1200 V/µs maximum — the limit is exceeded by over 13×.
This causes partial discharge and insulation breakdown within 1000 hours of continuous operation.
The Multilevel Solution
A 3-level topology reduces the voltage step to Vdc/2 = 400V, halving dv/dt to ~8 kV/µs.
The improved waveform reduces output THD by synthesizing a closer sine approximation.
Lower harmonic content reduces EMI and allows a smaller, lighter output filter.
