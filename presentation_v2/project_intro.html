Project Overview

In an EV traction inverter, the fundamental frequency is not fixed — it follows the vehicle operating condition because it is directly tied to motor speed via fe = (P/2) · nmech / 60.
Under city driving, the inverter operates at low fe with high current. Under highway driving, fe is higher but current is lower. The switching frequency can also be scheduled according to operating condition rather than kept constant, balancing efficiency, torque quality, and switching stress across the drive cycle.

Load:3-phase RL — R = 1.3 Ω, L = 2 mHDC Bus:800V battery sourceDeliverables:IEEE conference paper

Project Objectives
  • 1 Research and select a suitable three-level inverter topology, specifying components (voltage, current, frequency)
  • 2 Develop a detailed simulation model including DC source, switching devices, and capacitors
  • 3 Compare semiconductor temperature under adaptive fsw vs. constant 20 kHz baseline
  • 4 Analyze output current waveforms and verify expected THD performance
