Thermal Impact of Adaptive Schedule

Urban Tj reduction: Junction temperature drops from 63.2°C (fixed 20 kHz) to 53.5°C (adaptive 5 kHz) — a ΔTj of 9.7°C.
Peak torque Tj reduction: At 20 km/h, the temperature drops from 87.4°C to 71.3°C — a massive ΔTj of 16.2°C.
Thermal cycling amplitude over a mixed drive cycle is reduced by 53% (from 16.4°C to 7.7°C).
Per the Coffin-Manson fatigue model, this reduced thermal cycling directly and significantly extends solder fatigue lifetime.
All operating points remain well below the module's Tj_max of 175°C, providing >100°C of thermal margin.
