Simulated Adaptive Switching Frequency

An adaptive switching frequency schedule was fully implemented and validated within the dynamic time-domain simulation.

The methodology relies on maintaining a sufficient frequency modulation ratio (mf = fsw / fe) to ensure waveform quality while minimizing losses:

Urban Driving (Low fe): At 40 km/h, feis only 184 Hz. The simulated controller dynamically drops fswto5 kHzwhile keeping mf≈ 27. This yields a massive75% reductionin switching losses exactly when the phase current is highest (136 A).

Highway Driving (High fe): At 120 km/h, fereaches 552 Hz. The controller automatically increases fswto20 kHzto maintain mf≈ 36 and prevent distortion. Fortunately, the phase current is much lower (51 A), keeping absolute losses manageable.
