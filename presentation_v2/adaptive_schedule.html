Simulated Adaptive Switching Frequency

An adaptive switching frequency schedule was fully implemented and validated within the dynamic time-domain simulation.

The methodology relies on maintaining a sufficient frequency modulation ratio (mf = fsw / fe) to ensure waveform quality while minimizing losses:

Urban Driving (Low fe)
At 40 km/h, fe is only 184 Hz. The simulated controller dynamically drops fsw to 5 kHz while keeping mf ≈ 27. This yields a massive 75% reduction in switching losses exactly when the phase current is highest (136 A).
Highway Driving (High fe)
At 120 km/h, fe reaches 552 Hz. The controller automatically increases fsw to 20 kHz to maintain mf ≈ 36 and prevent distortion. Fortunately, the phase current is much lower (51 A), keeping absolute losses manageable.
