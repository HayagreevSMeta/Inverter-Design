Simulation Results

Time-domain simulation confirms proper 3-level output voltage synthesis at both urban and highway speeds.
Phase currents are clean sinusoids with THD < 2% (1.92% at 5 kHz urban, 1.13% at 20 kHz highway).
FC voltage is successfully maintained at 400V with ± 0.04V mean error using PD-PWM active state selection.
FC ripple: 18V simulated vs 20V analytical budget — excellent agreement with design calculations.
Pre-charge transient: 20ms linear ramp from 0V to 400V successfully executed before active switching commences.
