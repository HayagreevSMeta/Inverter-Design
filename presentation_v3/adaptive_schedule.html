Simulated Adaptive Switching Frequency

An adaptive switching frequency schedule was developed and fully implemented within the time-domain simulation. The core methodology relies on maintaining a sufficient frequency modulation ratio (mf = fsw/fe) to ensure waveform quality while minimizing losses.
Urban Driving (Low fe)
Because fe is low (184 Hz), the controller dynamically reduces fsw to 5 kHz while maintaining a high modulation ratio (mf ≈ 27). This reduces switching losses by 75% at the operating point where phase current is highest (136A).
