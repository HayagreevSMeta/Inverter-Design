Conclusion

Key Findings

  • Topology Validation: The 800V 3-Level Flying Capacitor topology successfully mitigates dv/dt stress and improves harmonic performance compared to 2L-VSI.
  • Loss Reduction: The simulated adaptive switching frequency schedule reduces urban switching losses by 75% (from 84.3W to 21.1W per phase).
  • Thermal Improvement: The adaptive schedule reduces thermal cycling amplitude by 53%, significantly extending the lifetime of the SiC power modules.

Limitations & Future Work

  • Ideal Switching: The current time-domain simulation employs ideal switching transitions.
  • Unmodeled Parasitics: The model does not currently account for device voltage drops, discrete dead-time distortion, DC bus ripple, or stray inductance.
  • Future Enhancements: Incorporating these non-idealities in future iterations would yield more conservative and hardware-accurate THD and loss estimates.
