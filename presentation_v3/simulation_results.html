Simulation Results

FC Voltage Balancing
  • Pre-charge Sequence: A 20ms linear ramp is implemented to prevent inrush currents during startup.
  • Natural Balancing: PD-PWM successfully keeps the flying capacitor voltage tightly regulated at exactly 400V.
  • Ripple Budget: The peak-to-peak voltage ripple is 18.0V, successfully meeting the strict 5% (20V) design constraint.
THD Validation
  • Harmonic Performance: The 3-level output inherently reduces the voltage step size, significantly improving power quality.
  • Urban Driving: At a 5 kHz switching frequency, the output Total Harmonic Distortion (THD) is 1.92%.
  • Highway Driving: At a 20 kHz switching frequency, the output THD is 1.13%.
Thermal Analysis
  • Foster Network: A 4-layer thermal model accurately captures the transient junction temperature dynamics.
  • Peak Temperature: The junction temperature (Tj) peaks at only 50.1°C under the adaptive schedule.
  • Safety Margin: This provides a massive 124.9°C safety margin to the 175°C semiconductor limit.
