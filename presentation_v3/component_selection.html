ECE 595 — PURDUE UNIVERSITY

Component Selection

Active Switch: SiC Module
  • Wolfspeed CAB450M12XM3: 1200V, 450A SiC half-bridge module.
  • Voltage Margin (1200V): Large safety margin over 400V stress; meets IPC-9592B derating at 33% use.
  • Current Margin (450A): ~3.3x margin vs. 136A peak phase current.
  • Thermal Stability: Positive temp. coeff. of Rds_on (3.3→6.6mΩ) helps prevent thermal runaway.
Flying Capacitor: Film Tech
  • Capacitance (680 µF): Keeps ripple within 5% (20V on 400V) at 5 kHz min switching.
  • Film vs. Electrolytic: Automotive-grade metallized polypropylene chosen.
  • Key Advantages: Very low ESR (<5 mΩ), long life, handles high-frequency ripple.
