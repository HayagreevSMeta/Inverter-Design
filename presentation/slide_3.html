two-level inverters at 800v produce 800v voltage steps that destroy motor insulation

the 800v challenge
A standard 2-level VSI at 800V switches the full bus voltage in under 50ns, generating dv/dt spikes exceeding 16 kV/µs.
Standard motor insulation is typically rated for a maximum of 1200V/µs, meaning the limit is exceeded by over 13x.
This extreme stress causes partial discharge and complete insulation breakdown within 1000 hours of continuous operation.
the multilevel solution
A 3-level topology inherently reduces the voltage step size to Vdc/2 = 400V, halving the dv/dt stress to ~8 kV/µs.
This topology significantly improves output THD (Total Harmonic Distortion) by synthesizing a closer approximation of a sine wave.
The improved harmonic profile reduces Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) and allows for a smaller, lighter output filter.
