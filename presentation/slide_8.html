at fixed 20 khz, switching losses reach 84w/phase during urban driving — 63% of total loss

Graovac-Purschel analytical method used for loss calculation:
Psw = Nc × (fsw/π) × Esw_ref × (Ipk/Iref) × (Vdc_sw/Vref)
Urban driving at 20 kHz: P_sw = 84.3 W/phase vs P_cond = 51.5 W/phase. Switching losses heavily dominate due to high phase currents (136A).
Highway driving at 20 kHz: P_sw = 31.7 W/phase vs P_cond = 5.6 W/phase. Both losses are relatively low due to reduced current (51A).
Key insight: Operating at 20 kHz is unnecessary at 40 km/h where the fundamental electrical frequency (fe) is only 184 Hz.
A minimum carrier ratio (mf = fsw/fe) of 27 at 5 kHz / 184 Hz is sufficient to maintain excellent THD while drastically cutting losses.
