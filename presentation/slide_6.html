1200v/450a sic module and 680µf film capacitor selected from first principles

Switch: Wolfspeed CAB450M12XM3 — 1200V rating provides a 50% margin over the 400V/switch stress. 450A rating offers a 3.3x margin over the 136A peak current.
SiC chosen over Si IGBT: Delivers 10x lower switching energy, eliminates tail current, and enables efficient 20 kHz operation.
Capacitor sizing: C = Ipk / (2 × fsw_min × ΔVfc) = 136A / (2 × 5kHz × 20V) = 680µF.
Film capacitor (polypropylene): Selected for ESR < 5mΩ, >100A RMS ripple capability, self-healing properties, and automotive-grade reliability.
Thermal: Rth_jc = 0.094 °C/W, modeled using a 4-layer Foster network for accurate transient thermal analysis.
