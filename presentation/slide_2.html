doubling bus voltage halves current, enabling faster charging and lighter cables

800V architecture enables ultra-fast DC charging at 350+ kW, reducing charge times from ~45 to under 20 minutes compared to 400V systems.
Halving current for the same power gives a ~75% reduction in I²R losses across inverter, motor windings and cabling.
Reduced current allows ~50% smaller copper cross-section, saving about 10–15 kg of vehicle weight.
Higher DC link voltage increases dv/dt stress on motor insulation, requiring advanced inverter topologies.
