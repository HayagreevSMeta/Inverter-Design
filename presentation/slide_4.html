flying capacitor chosen over npc for thermal symmetry and natural balancing

neutral point clamped (npc) limitations
Unequal loss distribution between inner and outer switches leads to asymmetric heating and complex cooling requirements.
Neutral point balance is notoriously difficult to maintain under transient EV loads, requiring complex control algorithms.
flying capacitor (fc) advantages
All 4 switches block identical voltage (Vdc/2 = 400V), ensuring symmetric thermal stress and allowing identical power modules.
Redundant switching states enable natural capacitor balancing regardless of the load power factor.
Trade-off: Requires a bulky capacitor and pre-charge circuit, which is acceptable in modern EVs where pre-charge contactors already exist.
