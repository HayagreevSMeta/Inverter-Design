WHY GRAOVAC-PURSCHEL APPLIES TO SiC:
- The method calculates average switching loss as: Psw = fsw/pi * Esw_ref * (Ipk/Iref) * (Vdc/Vref)
- This is topology-agnostic and device-agnostic — it only needs Esw from the datasheet
- For IGBTs: Esw scales non-linearly with current (quadratic)
- For SiC MOSFETs: Esw scales approximately linearly with current (simpler)
- The Gurpinar 2018 and Taha 2021 papers both use this exact approach with SiC devices
- The key adaptation: SiC conduction uses P = I^2 * Rds_on (resistive) instead of P = Vce_sat * I + r * I^2 (IGBT threshold + resistive)
