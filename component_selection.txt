SCALING FOR OUR APPLICATION:
   Our operating point: Vdc/2 = 400V (not 600V), Ipk ≈ 136A (not 450A)
   
   Voltage scaling: Esw ∝ V → factor = 400/600 = 0.667
   Current scaling (quadratic model): 
     Eon at 136A ≈ 25.4 * (0.1*(136/450)² + 0.8*(136/450) + 0.1) * (400/600)
                  ≈ 25.4 * (0.1*0.091 + 0.8*0.302 + 0.1) * 0.667
                  ≈ 25.4 * (0.0091 + 0.242 + 0.1) * 0.667
                  ≈ 25.4 * 0.351 * 0.667 = 5.95 mJ
     Eoff at 136A ≈ 7.51 * 0.351 * 0.667 = 1.76 mJ
     Total Esw ≈ 7.71 mJ per switching event at 136A, 400V
   
   Conduction loss at 136A:
     P_cond = Rds_on * I²_rms = 2.6e-3 * (136/√2)² = 2.6e-3 * 9248 = 24.0W per device
     With 2 devices conducting: 48W per phase (at 25°C)
     At 100°C: Rds_on ≈ 3.8 mΩ → P_cond = 3.8e-3 * 9248 = 35.1W per device → 70.2W per phase

2. FLYING CAPACITOR SELECTION:
   Requirements:
   - Voltage rating: ≥ 500V (FC holds 400V with margin)
   - Capacitance: 1.5 mF (from our sizing: C = Ipk/(2*fsw_min*ΔVfc) = 250/(2*5000*50) = 500µF minimum)
     Actually with our revised sizing at worst case: C = 136/(2*5000*5) = 2.72 mF for 5V ripple
     Or for 25V ripple (acceptable): C = 136/(2*5000*25) = 544 µF → round to 600µF
   - High ripple current capability (full load current flows through FC during zero states)
   - Low ESR for minimal heating
   - Automotive temperature range (-40 to +105°C minimum)
   
   OPTION A: KEMET C4AQ Series (AEC-Q200 qualified)
   - C4AQLBW5700A3LK: 70µF, 500V, polypropylene, AEC-Q200
   - Would need ~9 in parallel for 630µF → bulky but reliable
   - High ripple current per unit
   
   OPTION B: TDK B32776 Series (DC-Link, MKP)
   - B32776E5306K000: 30µF, 500V DC, polypropylene
   - Would need ~20 in parallel → too many
   - Better: B32776G8156K000: 15µF, 800V → wrong voltage class
   
   OPTION C: Custom film capacitor bank
   - For 1.5mF at 500V: likely need a custom capacitor assembly
   - TDK B32778 series goes up to 50µF at 900V
   - More realistic: use 4x 150µF 500V = 600µF per phase
   
   PRACTICAL INDUSTRY SOLUTION:
   For a 3-level FC inverter at this power level, the FC is typically:
   - A bank of parallel film capacitors on a custom busbar
   - Or a single large power film capacitor (e.g., from SBE or CDE)
   - Typical volume: ~0.5-1.0 L per phase for 600µF at 500V
   
   REVISED FC SIZING (more realistic):
   Using TDK FC sizing formula: C_FC = I_peak / (2 * f_sw * ΔV_FC)
   - At 5kHz (urban, worst case): C = 136 / (2 * 5000 * 20) = 680 µF for 20V ripple (5%)
   - At 20kHz (highway): C = 136 / (2 * 20000 * 20) = 170 µF (easily met)
   → Select C_FC = 680 µF (not 1.5 mF as in original design)
   → 1.5 mF is conservative but oversized (gives only 9V ripple at worst case)
   → For industry: 680µF is more practical, 1.5mF is academic overkill

3. DC-LINK CAPACITOR (MISSING FROM ORIGINAL DESIGN):
   Requirements:
   - Voltage: ≥ 900V (for 800V bus with margin)
   - Capacitance: sized for ripple current, not energy storage
   - Ripple current: I_ripple_rms ≈ 0.5 * I_load_rms * √(3) ≈ 0.5 * 96 * 1.73 ≈ 83A rms
   - ESR: low enough to limit voltage ripple to <2% (16V pp)
   - ESL: low enough for fast switching (< 20nH for SiC at 20kHz)
   
   SELECTION: TDK B32776G8306K000 (30µF, 800V, MKP)
   - Parallel 10x for 300µF total
   - Or use automotive-grade DC-link capacitor module
   - Industry standard: ~200-500µF for 100kW class inverter
   
   ALTERNATIVE: Film capacitor module (e.g., TDK EPCOS B25620 series)
   - Single module: 200µF, 900V, designed for inverter DC-link
   - Ripple current: 80-120A rms
   - This is what industry uses
